MailSend

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MailSend (“MailSend”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the MailSend application. MailSend is a job-search and email-assistance tool that helps users organize job information and prepare or send application emails.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your Google sign-in email address, name and profile image supplied by Google/Firebase Authentication.
  • Information you provide: candidate name, email, phone number, LinkedIn/GitHub links, profile text, job-search keywords, settings, uploaded resume files and screenshots.
  • Connected Gmail information: if you choose to connect Gmail, MailSend receives an authorization token and the email address of the connected account. The application requests the Gmail send permission to send messages you initiate or configure. It does not request permission to read your inbox.
  • Service credentials: API keys you enter for supported third-party services. These credentials and Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored by the application.
  • Application records: job details, generated email drafts, sending status, schedules and run history needed to provide the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, maintain your profile and preferences, process resumes and screenshots, find and organize job opportunities, generate application materials, run schedules you configure, send emails through the Gmail account you connect, troubleshoot the service and protect the application.

3. Third-party services

MailSend relies on third-party providers to operate. Depending on the features you use, information may be processed by:

  • Google/Firebase: Google sign-in, Gmail authorization and the Firestore database used for application records.
  • Supabase: private file storage for uploaded resumes and screenshots.
  • Groq: AI processing when you provide a Groq API key. Relevant resume text, screenshots or job/profile information may be sent to the AI provider to perform the feature you request.
  • HarvestAPI: job-search data retrieval when you provide and use a HarvestAPI key.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and security

Application records are stored in Firestore. Uploaded files are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets, separated by account. API keys and Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted by the application before they are saved. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure; keep your account credentials and API keys confidential and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

5. Gmail access and email sending

Connecting Gmail is optional. MailSend uses the Gmail send permission to send messages from the account you authorize. You choose whether to connect an account and configure sending or schedules. You can disconnect Gmail from the application. You can also revoke MailSend's access in your Google Account security settings.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account information and application records while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can remove certain items, such as resumes, screenshots, jobs and the Gmail connection, using available application controls. To request deletion of your account and associated data that cannot be deleted in the app, email malgopesayan65@gmail.com from your account email and include “MailSend data deletion request” in the subject. We may retain information where required for security, legal compliance or legitimate recordkeeping.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional profile information, API keys, files or Gmail authorization. You can disconnect Gmail and revoke third-party access. You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of your information.

8. Children's privacy

MailSend is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application changes. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact malgopesayan65@gmail.com.
